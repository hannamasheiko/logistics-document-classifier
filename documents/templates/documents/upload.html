Upload a PDF for classification

Accepted classes: INVOICE, BOL, POD, OTHER. At most 10 pages and 10,000,000 bytes. Both text-layer and scanned PDFs are supported: scanned pages go through local OCR, and when the text classification cannot accept a result, one visual fallback reviews the original page images. A document that cannot be classified with sufficient evidence ends as uncertain rather than a guess.
